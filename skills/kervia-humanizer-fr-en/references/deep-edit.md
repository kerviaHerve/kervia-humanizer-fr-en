# Deep editing, voice and drafting

Load only when structure, voice or an audit requires it. These are editorial decisions, not authorship tests.

## Diagnose before changing

| Actual problem | Useful intervention | Preserve when |
|---|---|---|
| Generic opening | Start with the relevant situation or answer | Context explains the reader's constraint |
| Inflated significance | State the available fact or consequence | Urgency or importance is supported |
| Borrowed authority | Attribute exactly or flag missing support | A source and its claim are provided |
| Meaningless contrast | State the useful positive claim | Both sides correct a real misconception |
| Repeated summaries | Give each passage a distinct job | A summary must stand alone |
| Lists hiding relationships | Explain cause, condition or choice | Inventory, procedure or comparison needs a list |
| Automatic triads or cloned paragraphs | Remove duplication; organise by substance | Items or records are genuinely comparable |
| Vague nouns and unknown responsibility | Recover the known actor and action | The actor is unknown or immaterial |
| Consequence tacked on by a participle | Check its evidence and scope | The source supports the connection |
| Terminology cycling | Keep one precise term | The concepts really differ |
| Theatrical fragments or aphorisms | Reconnect sentences around their point | Deliberate rhetoric has a purpose |
| Fake warmth or experience | Return to the supplied voice | The signatory actually supplied it |
| Decorative formatting | Keep emphasis that helps navigation or action | The channel has a useful convention |
| Chatbot wrapper in standalone copy | Remove the preamble and closing offer | A letter needs its greeting and sign-off |

## Voice

Read relevant samples for distance, vocabulary, syntax, argument, humour and uncertainty. A sample is evidence of style, not a stock of experiences to transfer. Keep voice distinct from the tone of this situation. Do not impose a universal conversational persona.

## Structure

Preserve the information contract. A revision is not an authorised summary. Reorder around the reader's task when the current sequence buries the answer. Keep context necessary for an independent document. A literary progression may be right for a narrative; a procedure may need steps rather than paragraphs.

## Drafting from notes

Separate supplied facts, verified facts, attributed opinions and unknowns. Select notes relevant to the mandate, then give paragraphs identifiable functions: answer, explanation, supplied example, condition, objection or next action. Do not manufacture controversy, testimony or missing detail to complete a template.

A requested creative or opinionated draft permits the requested invention or argument, not a fabricated real-world experience for an actual signatory. Label a proposed position or fictional example where that distinction matters.

## Assertion review

For a dense document, note actor, action, quantity and unit, comparison base, timing, conditions, exceptions, modality and attribution. Compare in both directions. Identical words and numbers can carry a changed meaning after reordering.

Use a local literal check if useful, then inspect the meaning yourself. One final review is enough unless new evidence reveals a material defect. Do not create a loop of increasingly “human” versions.
