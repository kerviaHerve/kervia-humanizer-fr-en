# Sources and scope

- [Humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer), MIT: editorial inspiration and a pinned benchmark baseline, credited separately.
- [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing): an evolving descriptive field guide, not a universal grammar or authorship test.
- [Reinhart et al., PNAS, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422455122): abstract and publication record on grammatical and rhetorical variation. Observed differences are not editing quotas.
- [Gude et al., ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1803/): abstract and publication record on diversity in English news, not all languages or genres.
- [Sourati et al., Nature Human Behaviour, 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-026-02550-0): public abstract on writing homogenisation; the published full text was subscription-only when consulted.
- [Zuccarino, Genesis 62, 2026](https://journals.openedition.org/genesis/11796): public abstract and excerpt on French fictional prose; full text announced for April 2027. No universal thresholds are inferred from its small corpus.
- [DITP: langage clair](https://www.modernisation.gouv.fr/simplifier-la-vie-des-usagers-et-des-agents/simplifier-le-langage-et-les-documents-administratifs): reader-centred principles in an administrative context.
- [OQLF: punctuation spacing](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/22039/la-typographie/espacement/espacement-avant-et-apres-les-signes-de-ponctuation-et-les-symboles): regional conventions, not a single rule for all French.
- [GOV.UK: clear language](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/writing-guidelines/clear-language/): useful language guidance within its own institutional remit.
- [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification): portable packaging and progressive disclosure.

Consulted during the initial research on 2026-11-01. The project turns these materials into bounded editing decisions, not a scientific detector or a claim of universally superior writing. House punctuation preferences are labelled as such. Recheck evolving pages before restating their current policies.
