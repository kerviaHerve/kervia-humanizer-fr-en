# Invoice Desk: unlocking a new chapter in invoice management

In today’s rapidly evolving business landscape, managing invoices has become a pivotal part of organisational success. Invoice Desk stands at the intersection of innovation and everyday work, offering a fresh perspective on how teams approach their documents. It is not merely a feature update; it is a whole new way of thinking about the administrative journey.

## A seamless foundation for your documents

Invoice Desk groups uploaded invoices by supplier. It accepts PDF files up to 15 MB each. Scanned documents that cannot be read remain in a manual review queue. The service does not make payments and has no direct connection to bank accounts. A person must review each invoice before it is marked as checked.

This carefully crafted approach underscores a commitment to putting people at the heart of the process. By bringing documents into one place, the service creates a shared starting point for teams looking to navigate the intricate world of administration. The real takeaway is that every document deserves attention.

## A clear plan for moving forward

The Team plan costs 29 USD per month, excluding sales tax, when billed monthly. It includes up to 50 invoices per month. The trial lasts 14 days and does not require a payment card. The Free plan does not include supplier grouping. These conditions apply to the web service; no desktop application is offered.

Here is the thing: clear boundaries are not simply restrictions. They are the foundation of a thoughtful experience, helping teams align expectations and move forward with confidence. This is where purpose meets practicality in a meaningful way.

## Looking ahead with confidence

Exports are available only in CSV format. A date for spreadsheet exports has not been announced. The service can flag a possible duplicate, but that flag is not confirmation that an invoice was submitted twice. Users must check it themselves.

Ultimately, Invoice Desk represents more than a place for documents. It is a testament to the enduring value of a considered workflow, bringing clarity, consistency and connection to the administrative landscape. The future of invoice management starts with a single step.
